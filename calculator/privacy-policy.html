Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026. This Privacy Policy applies to Calculator - All in One & Unit Converter developed by Quizity ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to transparency and respect your personal privacy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Calculator is built on an offline-first architecture. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No User Account: You do not need an account, login, phone number, or email address to use the app.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: We do not include third-party trackers, behavioral profiling libraries, or advertising identifiers.
  • No Cloud Sync of Numbers: All calculations, math tape history, custom formula inputs, loan schedules, BMI records, and favorite calculator bookmarks remain strictly on your local device.

2. Network & Internet Permission

The app requests the standard Android INTERNET permission solely for one feature:

  • Live Currency Converter: Fetching up-to-date foreign exchange rates from publicly available financial currency rate feeds over encrypted HTTPS. No user IDs, device serial numbers, or calculation records are ever transmitted with these rate requests.

3. Local Device Storage

The app uses Android's local preferences (SharedPreferences) to remember:

  • Your designated default home screen calculator.
  • Your pinned/favorite calculator list.
  • Recent calculation history for convenient review and reuse.

This data never leaves your physical phone.

4. Third-Party Services

The application operates independently without third-party ad networks or surveillance SDKs. Any external links (such as sharing a calculation result via your device's native share sheet) are controlled entirely by you.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not maintain external servers with your data, clearing the app storage in your Android settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally cached preferences and history.

6. Children's Privacy

Calculator is a general utility and educational tool safe for all audiences. It does not target or knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age.
