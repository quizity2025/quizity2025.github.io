Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026. Pocket Wallet AI is designed to keep personal finance records on your device while clearly disclosing the optional Google services used by the app. Pocket Wallet AI does not display advertisements.

Information stored on your device

Transactions, accounts, budgets, categories, goals, bills, subscriptions, investment records, shared-expense records, settings, and generated insights are stored in the app's local database. We do not operate a proprietary server that stores your personal finance ledger.

Notification access

If you explicitly enable Android notification access, the app can detect supported banking and UPI payment alerts and suggest expense or income entries. Notification text is parsed on your device. Pocket Wallet AI does not read your SMS inbox, call history, or unrelated private messages, and does not send notification contents to our servers.

Camera and receipt scanning

Camera access is requested only when you choose receipt scanning. Receipt text recognition is performed on device to help prefill transaction details. You can deny camera permission and continue using manual entry.

Firebase services

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config. These services may process app interactions, installation or device identifiers, diagnostic data, crash logs, and app configuration information. Analytics and crash events are designed not to include raw transaction notes, balances, holdings, receipt contents, or notification text.

Google Drive and exports

Exports and backups occur only when you request them. Files saved to device storage or shared with another app are controlled by you. If you use Google Drive backup, Google authentication and Drive services process the account and backup file under Google's privacy terms. We do not receive your Google password.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal finance records. Data may be processed by Google services used for analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration, authentication, Play services, or optional Drive backup, subject to their respective terms. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

Retention and deletion

Local finance data remains on your device until you delete records, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported and Drive backup files remain in destinations you control until you delete them. Google service data is retained according to Google's applicable retention policies.

Security and your choices

The app supports device security controls such as biometrics where available. You can manage camera, notification, notification-access, and other permissions in Android Settings. No security measure is absolute, so protect your device and exported backup files.

Children

Pocket Wallet AI is a personal finance utility intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect a child's personal finance information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to quizity2025@gmail.com.
