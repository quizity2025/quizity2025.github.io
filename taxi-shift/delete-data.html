01

Start over with 500 coins

In versions with Cloud Save, open Settings > Common > Cloud Save & Progress > Reset Progress while connected to Google Play Games and the internet. Confirm the reset. It replaces local and cloud progression with 500 coins and the starter car. Owned cars, upgrades, styles, career, reward claims and timers restart. This cannot be undone. It keeps the account link for future backups and does not erase permanent Google Play Games badges or optional reports.

02

Request deletion of cloud data

Email quizity2025@gmail.com with the subject Taxi Shift data deletion. Include your Cloud Save player ID if available and explain whether you want the cloud game profile, its Firebase Authentication record, support correspondence or all of these removed. We may verify account ownership before deleting information we control. A parent or guardian may request this for their child. Do not send passwords or sensitive information about a child. Disconnect Google Play Games for Taxi Shift and stop using Cloud Save if you do not want a new profile to be created after deletion. We explain any records that must be retained for legal obligations or to document the request.

03

Remove data stored on your phone

Android Settings > Apps > Taxi Shift > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the game, removes local app data subject to your device backup settings. It does not delete an account-linked cloud save. Reconnecting the same Google Play Games account can restore the last successful backup. Progress that was never backed up cannot be recovered from our cloud service.

04

Google Play Games and optional reports

For permanent Google Play badges, use Google Play Games settings > Delete Play Games account & data > Delete individual game data > Taxi Shift. This is separate from cloud-wallet deletion and from deleting your Google account. Optional gameplay and crash reports can be switched off in Settings > About > Gameplay & Crash Reports. This stops future optional reporting, not reports already uploaded. Contact us for deletion assistance; technical identifiers may be needed to locate reports because we do not attach your email or Google Play identity to custom gameplay reports.

05

Advertising-provider information

Advertising providers manage their technical and ad-delivery information separately. Use Unity privacy request options and Google privacy controls, or contact Quizity for help finding the appropriate provider. Provider retention and deletion exceptions apply.
