Delete your data

Taxi Shift has no separate game account. Choose the data you want to remove.

01

Request deletion from Quizity

Email quizity2025@gmail.com with the subject Taxi Shift data deletion. Tell us which support messages or other information you want removed. A parent or guardian can request this for their child. Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive information about a child.

We may need to verify a request before deleting information we control. We delete identifiable support information when it is no longer needed, except records we must retain for legal obligations or to document the request; we explain any applicable exception in our reply. Quizity has no server holding your driving progress.

02

Remove progress stored on your phone

Open Android Settings > Apps > Taxi Shift > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the game. This removes local coins, cars, upgrades, health, fuel and preferences. These cannot be restored by Quizity. Your phone's backup service may separately retain a backup; manage it in your device or Google backup settings.

03

Google Play Games and reports

For Google Play achievements, open Google Play Games settings > Delete Play Games account & data > Delete individual game data, then choose Taxi Shift. This is separate from deleting your Google account.

Optional gameplay and crash reports can be switched off in Taxi Shift Settings > About > Gameplay & Crash Reports. This stops future optional reporting; it does not erase reports already uploaded. Contact us for deletion assistance. Technical identifiers may be needed to locate an uploaded report; we do not attach your email or Google Play identity to gameplay reports. Data that cannot be linked back to an individual cannot be identified through an email address alone.

04

Data handled by advertising providers

Ad providers independently process technical and ad-delivery information. Use Unity's privacy request options and Google's privacy controls for their data. Provider retention periods and deletion exceptions apply. You can also contact Quizity for help identifying the appropriate provider.
