01

Who we are

Taxi Shift City Adventures (Android package com.subhu.taxinew) is developed and published by Quizity. This notice covers the game, its optional online services, support messages, and these legal pages. Contact us using the details below.

02

Your progress and settings

The game saves coins, owned cars, upgrades, car health and fuel, achievements, event eligibility, rewarded-benefit receipts, and your control, sound and notification preferences on your device. Quizity does not operate a server that stores your city-driving progress. Android or a device backup service may separately back up or restore app data according to your device settings. Google Play achievements are described separately below.

03

Google Play Games

The game attempts Google Play Games sign-in when it launches. You can continue without connecting if sign-in is unavailable or you decline, and try Connect Google Play in Settings later. When connected, the Google Play Games SDK handles authentication and exchanges achievement identifiers and progress with Google. Google manages your Play Games profile and credentials; the game does not ask you to enter or store your Google password. Local weekly coin achievements remain separate from lifetime Google Play achievements. Manage your Play Games profile, permissions and game-data deletion through Google Play Games and your Google account.

04

Optional rewarded ads

Taxi Shift uses Unity LevelPlay to offer optional video ads for bonuses and assistance. Ad services can initialize and request or cache ads when the game is online, before you choose to watch one. Configured advertising providers include Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Google AdMob; actual delivery depends on network configuration and availability. Providers may process an IP address, approximate region inferred from it, device/app information, language, diagnostic data, and ad-request, viewing and reward events for delivery, security, measurement and fraud prevention. The game uses child-directed settings, requests restricted/non-personalized ads, and removes the Android advertising-ID permission. These settings do not mean that ad providers receive no technical data. Provider privacy notices explain their processing and choices.

05

Children and families

The game includes players under 13. The current build applies child-directed advertising settings to all players, including users of unknown age. It does not ask for a date of birth or create an in-game social profile, chat, or public messaging feature. We do not use local driving progress to build behavioural advertising profiles. Parents and guardians can manage optional Google Play features and Android notification permissions for their child. A parent or guardian may ask us to review, correct, or delete information Quizity holds about their child, or to stop further collection that we control. We will take reasonable steps to verify the request without asking for unnecessary personal information. If we learn that support correspondence contains a child's information we should not retain, we will delete it unless retention is legally necessary.

06

Permissions and local reminders

Internet/network access supports ads and Google Play services. Notification permission is optional: reminders are scheduled locally for daily play, free-race availability and free vehicle recovery. Reminder timing uses your device clock and time zone; it does not require GPS or a location permission. Turn reminders off in Android app-notification settings. The game's camera and mirror are virtual game cameras: the game does not request access to your real camera, microphone, contacts or precise device location.

07

Device performance and support

The game reads device capability and thermal/performance information to choose graphics settings and manage rendering. Testing tools can create diagnostic files locally when explicitly started. Those files are not automatically uploaded to Quizity; if you choose to export or send them, review them first. If you email support, your email provider delivers your address, message and any attachments to us. We use that correspondence to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send sensitive information about a child.

08

Service providers and processing locations

Google and the advertising providers process information needed for their services under their own privacy notices. We do not sell local game-save data or send your local wallet and car inventory to an advertising network. Providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their notices describe applicable safeguards, retention and privacy choices. We may disclose support correspondence when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect safety and legal rights.

09

Retention and deletion

Local saves and preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall, subject to any separate device-backup restoration. Clearing storage can permanently remove coins, cars and other progress; the game does not promise cloud restoration of these items. Google Play achievement data and ad-provider data follow those providers' retention and deletion processes. Quizity keeps support messages only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of information held by Quizity by email.

10

Your choices and rights

You can play the core driving game without an internet connection, decline rewarded ads, manage notifications in Android, and manage Google Play Games access through Google. Local progress and privacy/terms notices remain available offline. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact Quizity for information we control; use the provider links for data controlled by those services. We may need to verify your request.

11

This legal website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive connection information such as your IP address and request logs when you visit. Quizity has added no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies or external font services to the Taxi Shift legal pages. External links open services with their own policies.

12

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the game or its data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be explained through an appropriate in-game or store notice where required. The game includes a local copy, so an older app version may contain an older notice; the online page shows the latest published notice.
